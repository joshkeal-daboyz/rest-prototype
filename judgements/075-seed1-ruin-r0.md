## Must fix
- S1 (2): Spots a, b and d have no added growth at all. They need plants over road edges, pavements, walls and roofs, thicker in some stretches than others. In c, the mounds and saplings need to spread onto the pavements and walls rather than sit only on the road.
- S2 (2): In the night frames, the lime-green sapling crowns in c, the pale white towers on the roofs in a and d, the orange tarps and the bright poster columns stand out as the lightest, off-palette things in the frame. They need to sink into deep blues and greens, no lighter than the walls around them.
- S3 (1): The big orange tarps over the roofs in a, b and d and the orange pods on the left pavement in b are large saturated pieces that grab the eye first. They need to lose their saturation or shrink to small touches, so the street keeps room for one saturated ribbon.
- S4 (2): The tarp draped over the top right roof edge in a is bigger than a window bay and turns the corner into a soft blob. The row of tarps along the left roofline in d and the tall striped towers on the right in d make the skyline ragged. Roof additions need to be small against the blocks and leave the roofline square.
- S5 (6): In a, the long grey wall along the right pavement hides the whole right-hand ground floor, where the reference shows shops and an awning. In c, the tall sapling stands in front of the shop under the beer sign. The frontage on both sides needs to read at a glance.
- S8 (3): The lime saplings in c, the blue label on the right wall in b, the pink label and cyan strips in d, the cyan kiosk panel in c and the pale towers in d all read as lit at night. Nothing an era added should look like it gives off light.

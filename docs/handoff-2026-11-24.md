## Working rules Josh set in this stretch

- **Every question is a card** (AskUserQuestion). His card words go in merge commits.
- **At most two agents at a time.** His PC slowed badly and lost power on 24 Nov.
- **No headless browsers.** Headless Edge left about 12 swiftshader processes running. For high-res shots, iframe the page in the browser pane, patch `getContext` to `preserveDrawingBuffer: true`, then `toDataURL` and POST it to a small local receiver.
- **Tests:** builders and reviewers run them in the foreground, with at most 2 vitest workers, and leave nothing running.
- **CI is the gate.** A 5 s timeout that passes on a re-run is noise; real failures go in `docs/ci-times.md`. Measure one PR in ten (the next is after #60).
- **Branch deletion is Josh's word on a card** after each merge: archive tag, unlink junctions, remove the worktree, delete the branch.
- **Worktrees** are `D:\rest-081<section>`, with junctions for `node_modules` and `assets/private/{out,calibration}`, plus `in` for Blender work.
- **Dev servers** go in `.claude/launch.json` temporarily; `git checkout .claude/launch.json` after. Never commit a `-am` that sweeps it in.
- **Synty licence:** GLBs and pack files stay in git-ignored `assets/private` and are never committed or shipped.

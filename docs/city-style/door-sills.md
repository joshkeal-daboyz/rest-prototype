## Rules

### 16.10 Door sills (ticket 055 F)

How high a building's door stands over the walk in front of it, and what is done about it. `sills.ts` holds the measured table, the plan and the measuring function (`measureDoor`), `models3d.ts` draws the result (the sink, the dressing's offset, the steps), and `sills.test.ts` pins every value and re-measures them from the converted GLBs where they are on the machine.

- **Rule [D-055h]**: a building's door sill is measured, one number for each model, and a building whose sill is out of reach of the walk is sunk until its door meets it.
- **Rule [D-055h]**: the rule applies everywhere, on the tile city and on the ladder maps alike: they are the same buildings, and a door nobody can reach is wrong on both. On the ladder maps only building and stack heights, the dressing's heights on the blocks that sink, and the added steps change; the layout, the packer, the furniture, the open lots, the tiles, picking, the Relight and the playtest snapshots are as they were. The sink is applied when drawing (`applyModels`), never in the plans.
- **Rule [D-055h]**: a sill up to `SILL_MAX_M` is left alone: a door step.
- **Rule [D-055h]**: sink first. A house (a whole model) sinks by its sill, but no further than leaves its lowest ground-floor window `SILL_WINDOW_CLEAR_M` over the walk; if that leaves more than `SILL_MAX_M` to climb, the Town Pack's concrete steps stand at its door, outside its front face and tucked `STEPS_TUCK_M` into it, scaled in y alone (`STEPS_SCALE_Y`) until their top (`STEPS_RISE_M` at scale 1) meets the door to within `STEPS_FIT_M`. A rise under the steps' smallest is made by sinking less, so the steps still meet the door. A stack (a City Pack building, a terrace block or a tall-lot stack) always sinks, as one, by the largest sill of its ground pieces (the base, door and corner pieces, role "base"), never takes steps (a terrace's front is the walk, and steps there would stand in the walkers' way), and takes its dressing (glass, awnings, signs, fire escapes, roof props) down with it. Its ground pieces are then lifted one by one so each door meets the walk (`docs/city-style/sill-lift.md`). Furniture, open lots, tiles and lot placeholders do not move.
- **Rule [D-055h]**: the steps are `STEPS_KEY`, the Town Pack's `SM_Bld_House_Concrete_Steps_01`, 1.2 x 0.5 x 0.9 m (`STEPS_W_M` x `STEPS_RISE_M` x `STEPS_D_M`): a second texture atlas beside the houses that already use it, drawn as one InstancedMesh and never picked. With the model absent (a fresh checkout, CI), the steps are one InstancedMesh of plain boxes of the same size in the lot's ground colour (`YARD`). Puzzle pages ask for the steps with the town; the tile city does not, because it has no house and would otherwise fetch the Town Pack's 110 MB for nothing. The Town Pack's `SM_Bld_House_DeckSteps_01` is broken (16.6).
- **Default (062)**: every placeable building has a sill. A key whose first door floor starts further back than `SILL_DEPTH_M` (a recessed porch, a set-back shop door) is measured with its own search depth, `SILL_DEPTH_BY_KEY` (a depth and a reason each, rounded up from the floor's measured start to a tenth of a metre, never over `SILL_DEPTH_MAX_M`).
- **Default (062)**: beyond `SILL_DEPTH_MAX_M`, or where there is no front floor, the sill is typed in with its reason (the `why` of its entry), or the key is in `NO_STREET_DOOR` with a reason, sill 0, drawn unchanged. `SM_Bld_House_Preset_Garage_01` is typed in and sinks like the houses beside it, by the same sill, so no plinth is left beside them.
- **Default (062)**: a window is read from the model where it has no glass material. `window_m` is the lowest front-facing face that is recessed at least `RECESS_MIN_M` behind the front plane, of at least `RECESS_AREA_M2` (faces on one plane within `RECESS_FLAT_M`, touching within `RECESS_JOIN_M`, are one surface), no deeper than `WINDOW_DEPTH_M`, with its lowest edge at least `RECESS_ABOVE_FLOOR_M` above the door floor, and not wholly within `RECESS_DOOR_HALF_M` either side of the door's centre (that is the door's leaf). Where none can be read, the entry has no front window and is listed in `NO_FRONT_WINDOW` with a reason. A City Pack ground piece is a stack's and has no window check; its window is the first floor's (`WINDOW_SILL_M`).
- **Default (062)**: a front step is never buried. `step_m` is the top of the lowest upward-facing level in front of the door floor (at least `STEP_MIN_M` high and `STEP_BELOW_FLOOR_M` under the floor, no further back than the floor starts). A house sinks no further than leaves that step at the walk, and the rest is made up by the concrete steps, by the sink-first rule above (the steps are scaled in y alone, and a rise under their smallest is made by sinking less).

`NO_STREET_DOOR` and the typed-in entries are the only keys that are not measured within `SILL_DEPTH_MAX_M`. A City Pack ground piece's `step_m` is not kept in the table (a stack never takes steps). The table is in `docs/city-style/sill-lift.md`; every placeable key is in it or in `NO_STREET_DOOR`.

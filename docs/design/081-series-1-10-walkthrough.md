# Series v1, puzzles 1 to 10: how to solve each

Played on main c77066c, 26 Nov 2026. Each set of steps is the analyzer's shortest win, which I replayed in the game, and each one won. "Close" a breaker means turn it on and "open" means turn it off. To mend a fault, click it with the part in hand. Each section also names the trap, meaning the wrong move each puzzle is built to tempt.

## 01 · First light
Teaches: the breaker.

| Step | Move |
|---|---|
| 1 | Close the shrine's breaker, the one beside the gold shrine. |

- Wins at 4 of 5 load. Only one move is needed; the other breaker is optional.
- Trap: the other breaker, up the side street, lights a home. That does no harm.

## 02 · This way or that
Teaches: the switch.

| Step | Move |
|---|---|
| 1 | Throw the corner switch, the one by the feed. |
| 2 | Throw the pump switch, down the side street. |

- Wins at 3 of 4.
- Trap: throw the pump switch first and nothing changes, because no current reaches it yet. Its number reads +0.

## 03 · Not enough for all
Teaches: capacity and brownout.

| Step | Move |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open the row breaker (−3), which cuts the home and the kiln. |

- It starts at 9 of 6 in brownout and wins at 6 of 6.
- Trap: shedding the two small houses (−1, −2, both red) still leaves 7 of 6. Opening the infirmary's own breaker (−6) cuts the target.

## 04 · The spark
Teaches: a damaged cable only shows itself under current.

| Step | Move |
|---|---|
| 1 | Throw the switch toward the granary. A spark appears on the dark line. |
| 2 | Mend the spark with the wire. |

- Wins at 2 of 3.
- Trap: clicking the dark line before the switch does nothing, because the fault can't be read yet.

## 05 · Not that one
Teaches: scarce parts.

| Step | Move |
|---|---|
| 1 | Close the lift's breaker. |
| 2 | Repair the dead junction by the lift, which takes both wires. |

- Wins at 2 of 3.
- Trap: mending the loud spark round the corner uses one wire. It's on a neighbour's line, and then the junction needs 2 wires and you have only 1. One Undo recovers.

## 06 · Behind the fuse
Teaches: faults hide faults (the first fuse box and the first dead bulb).

| Step | Move |
|---|---|
| 1 | Close the bath house breaker. |
| 2 | Fit the fuse in the blown fuse box (0 of 3). |
| 3 | Fit the bulb in the bath house. |

- Wins at 7 of 8.
- Trap: a home with live wiring and dark windows asks for your one bulb. The bath house, behind the blown fuse, needs it. If you only notice late, it takes up to 3 Undos.

## 07 · The long way
Teaches: reroute instead of a repair you can't afford.

| Step | Move |
|---|---|
| 1 | Throw the corner switch onto the long way round the block. |
| 2 | Close the breaker on the long way. A spark shows one span on. |
| 3 | Mend that spark with the wire. |

- Wins at 15 of 16.
- Trap: the dead junction on the short way needs 2 wires and you have 1, so the game refuses the repair.

## 08 · The small box
Teaches: a fuse rating is a local limit, and a blow costs a part.

| Step | Move |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open the outside home's breaker (−1). |
| 2 | Open the mill's breaker. The mill is past the target, behind the box. |
| 3 | Close the cold store's breaker. |

- Wins at 9 of 9, with the box at 6 of 6.
- Trap: shedding only the outside home doesn't help the box, which then blows. The breaker that lights the target reads red.

## 09 · Two ways round
Teaches: the ring, meaning which end feeds and which side of each break a target is on.

| Step | Move |
|---|---|
| 1 | Throw the corner switch north. The shrine lights. |
| 2 | Open the kiln breaker, to shed the kiln and its houses. |
| 3 | Mend the north spark with the wire. The meeting hall lights. |

- Wins at 10 of 12.
- Trap: mending the spark by the lit homes lights one target and strands the shrine behind the second break. One Undo recovers.

## 10 · Count the dark
Teaches: a bulb is a load event.

| Step | Move |
|---|---|
| 1 | Close the archive's breaker. It reads +2 red, but nothing blows, because the building is dead. |
| 2 | Open the tannery's breaker. The tannery is past the archive on the same wire. |
| 3 | Fit the fuse in the fuse box. |
| 4 | Fit the bulb in the archive. |

- Wins at 8 of 9, with the box at 5 of 5.
- Trap: with the tannery still on, the bulb brings 2 more load at once and blows the box.
- It has the same shape as 08: shed the building past the target, then close the target's breaker.

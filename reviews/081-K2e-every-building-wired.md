## What the ticket asked for, as I understood it
At stage 6, every building with a front column on a street gets one drop: from its own pole line, else from a crossing pole on its own block side within 7.5 m, else across the street from that street's pole pavement (up to 23.5 m, `street` set). CK9's check is turned round to match. CK7's 4.2 m floor binds drops and 059's 5.5 m no longer does. CK17d keeps roof spans 1 m from drops, except drops from the span's own end poles, judged from the pole axis. The pins that move are re-taken.
